# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in tpt-reficon, in issues, discussions, or any other
project space, is expected to treat others with respect and good faith. Disagree
about the technical merits freely, and keep the criticism aimed at the work, not
at people.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination
based on who someone is, publishing someone's private information without their
consent, and conduct that a reasonable person would find hostile.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), or by contacting the maintainers through the
repository. Reports are read by the maintainers, who decide on a response,
which may include removing content, a warning, or a ban from the project spaces.
